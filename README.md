# Motor Insurance Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing motor insurance business performance using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into premium collections, claims payments, vehicle category performance and profitability.

The objective is to identify high-risk vehicle segments, understand claims patterns and support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
- Business Analysis

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Business Overview
- Total Premium and Total Policies
- Total Claims Paid
- Average Premium and Average Claim Payment
- Claims-to-Premium Ratio
- Yearly Premium and Claims Trends
- Premium vs Claims Comparison

### 2. Segment Performance
- Premium and Claims by Vehicle Category
- Claims-to-Premium Ratio by Vehicle Type
- Premium Less Claims Analysis

### 3. Detailed Profitability Analysis
- Vehicle-wise Claims Ratio
- Total Claims Paid
- Total Premium
- Premium Less Claims
- Conditional Formatting for Performance Comparison

## Key Business Insights

1. Overall claims-to-premium ratio stands at 227.3%, indicating claims payments significantly exceed premium collections.
2. Trucks contribute approximately $4.3bn in claims and have the largest negative premium-minus-claims result of approximately $2.9bn.
3. Trailers and semitrailers record the highest claims-to-premium ratio at 481.5%.
4. Motorcycles (35%) and tractors (29.8%) show comparatively lower claims ratios.
5. Claims peaked during 2015–2016, while premiums and claims declined sharply in 2018.

## Business Recommendations

- Review premium pricing and underwriting practices for high-claims vehicle categories.
- Investigate the reasons behind unusually high truck and trailer claims.
- Monitor claims ratios regularly to identify emerging risks.
- Analyse better-performing vehicle segments for potential business opportunities.
