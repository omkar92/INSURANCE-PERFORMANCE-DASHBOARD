## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyse motor insurance business performance and identify patterns in premium collections, claims payments and vehicle-wise financial performance.

Using Power BI, I developed an interactive three-page dashboard to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), compare vehicle categories, analyse historical trends and identify segments with a high claims burden.

The analysis aims to support data-driven decisions around underwriting practices, premium adequacy and claims management.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

The dashboard highlights the following findings:

| Business Metric | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Overall Claims Ratio | 227.3% |
| Highest Claims Category | Trucks – approximately $4.3bn |
| Highest Claims Ratio | Trailers and Semitrailers – 481.5% |
| Truck Premium Less Claims | Approximately -$2.9bn |
| Motorcycle Claims Ratio | 35% |
| Tractor Claims Ratio | 29.8% |

### Major Findings

1. **High Claims Exposure:** The overall claims-to-premium ratio of 227.3% indicates that claims payments significantly exceed premium collections.

2. **Truck Segment:** Trucks contribute the highest absolute claims amount and represent the largest negative premium-minus-claims result.

3. **Trailer Segment:** Trailers and semitrailers record the highest claims ratio at 481.5%, indicating a substantial claims burden relative to premiums.

4. **Better-Performing Segments:** Motorcycles and tractors show comparatively lower claims ratios, making them interesting segments for further investigation.

5. **Historical Trends:** Claims peaked during 2015–2016, while both premiums and claims declined sharply in 2018.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve insurance portfolio performance:

- **Review Underwriting Practices:** Investigate high-claims vehicle categories, particularly trucks and trailers.
- **Premium Adequacy:** Evaluate whether premium pricing adequately reflects the risk associated with different vehicle categories.
- **Claims Monitoring:** Monitor claims ratios regularly to identify emerging risks.
- **Segment Analysis:** Investigate better-performing categories to understand their comparatively favourable claims patterns.
- **Historical Performance:** Examine yearly variations in premiums and claims to identify changes in business exposure.

## 📐 Key Business Metrics

The following measures were used to evaluate insurance performance:

| KPI | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Total Premium | Sum of Premium Amount |
| Total Claims Paid | Sum of Claims Paid |
| Total Policies | Count of Policies |
| Average Premium | Total Premium / Total Policies |
| Average Claim Payment | Total Claims Paid / Claim Count |
| Claims-to-Premium Ratio | Total Claims Paid / Total Premium × 100 |
| Premium Less Claims | Total Premium - Total Claims Paid |

**Note:** Premium Less Claims represents a simplified claims margin, not actual underwriting profit. Operating expenses and other income are not included.
